## 👑 Custom / Any Guest Name

You can also use camelCase or custom names:
* `http://localhost:4321/johnDoe` -> **Hi, John Doe!**
* `http://localhost:4321/sarah-smith` -> **Hi, Sarah Smith!**
* `http://localhost:4321/` -> Default Invitation
